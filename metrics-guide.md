# Metrics guide

What to measure, where to find it in LinkedIn, how to calculate it, and what to do with the answer.

## Where to find the numbers

| Metric | Where in LinkedIn |
|---|---|
| Impressions, members reached | Profile → Analytics → Content (pick the date range) |
| In-network vs out-of-network | Content analytics → Discovery |
| Reactions, comments, reposts, saves, sends | Content analytics → Engagement, or open each post → "View analytics" |
| Profile viewers | Profile → Analytics → Profile viewers |
| Followers, new followers | Profile → Analytics → Audience |
| Custom button clicks | Content analytics → Engagement (Premium) |
| Search appearances | Profile → Analytics → Search appearances |

LinkedIn analytics change from time to time. If a label has moved, look under your profile's Analytics section.

## Rates to calculate

| Rate | Formula | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Engagement rate** | (reactions + comments + reposts + saves + sends) ÷ impressions | Is the content interesting to the people who see it? |
| **Comments per 100 reactions** | comments ÷ reactions × 100 | Are people talking back, or just liking? |
| **Out-of-network share** | out-of-network impressions ÷ total impressions | Is the content reaching new people? |
| **Reach vs followers** | members reached ÷ followers | Are posts travelling beyond your audience? |
| **Profile view rate** | profile viewers ÷ members reached × 1,000 | Are readers curious about the person? (per 1,000 reached) |
| **Follower conversion** | new followers ÷ profile viewers | Does the profile turn visitors into followers? |
| **Week-over-week change** | (this week − last week) ÷ last week | Is it growing? |

All of these are calculated for you in [`ghostwriting-tracker.xlsx`](ghostwriting-tracker.xlsx).

## Worked example: the real week from the case study

| Number | Value |
|---|---|
| Impressions | 73,322 (previous week ~2,300) |
| Members reached | 48,750 |
| Followers | 16,992 |
| Out-of-network share | 93% |
| Reactions / comments / saves / sends / reposts | 120 / 64 / 10 / 3 / 0 |
| Profile viewers | 263 (+298%) |
| Custom button clicks | 13 |

| Rate | Calculation | Result | What it told us |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engagement rate | 197 ÷ 73,322 | 0.27% | Normal for very wide reach: most viewers were new |
| Comments per 100 reactions | 64 ÷ 120 × 100 | **53** | Excellent: people were talking back |
| Reach vs followers | 48,750 ÷ 16,992 | **2.9x** | Posts travelled far beyond his followers |
| Week-over-week impressions | (73,322 − 2,300) ÷ 2,300 | **+3,088%** (LinkedIn shows 3,077% from exact figures) | A breakout week |
| Follower growth | under 1% | Low | **Action:** add a follow CTA and use the profile button |

## Rules of thumb (not hard benchmarks)

Results vary a lot by audience size and niche. Use these as starting points and compare the founder against their own past weeks.

| Signal | Healthy | Worth acting on |
|---|---|---|
| Comments per 100 reactions | 20+ (50+ is excellent) | Under 10: posts are "nod and scroll". Add questions, opinions, stories |
| Out-of-network share | 50%+ | Under 30%: posts aren't travelling. Work on hooks and broader topics |
| Saves | Some saves on how-to posts | Zero saves on how-to posts: make them more practical, or a carousel |
| Profile viewers | Rising after strong weeks | Flat despite reach: the hook-to-person link is weak; mention the founder's work more |
| Follower conversion | Rising | High reach, flat followers: fix the profile and add follow CTAs |

## The weekly review (10 minutes)

1. Log this week's numbers in the tracker.
2. Find the best post by **comments** and by **profile views** (not impressions).
3. Write one line: why did it work? (topic, hook, format, timing)
4. Find the weakest post. Write one line: what would you change?
5. Add 2–3 new topics to the bank based on what worked.

## The monthly decision

After 4 weeks, use the tracker's "By pillar" sheet:
- Give **more slots** to the pillar with the most comments and profile views per post.
- Give **fewer slots** to the pillar with the lowest, but don't drop it in one month.
- Repeat the top 3 hooks' **patterns** (not the same words).
