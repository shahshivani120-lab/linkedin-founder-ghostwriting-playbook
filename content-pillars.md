# Content pillars and calendar

Pillars are the 3–4 themes the founder will be known for. They keep content varied but focused, so the right people learn what the founder is an expert in.

## Step 1: choose pillars

A good pillar sits where three things overlap:

1. **The founder knows it deeply** (they have stories and opinions)
2. **The buyer cares about it** (it's tied to a problem they have)
3. **It leads to the business** (someone who likes this content could become a customer)

| Pillar type | What it does | Example (IT services founder) |
|---|---|---|
| **Expertise** | Shows you know the craft | Cloud costs, security, uptime |
| **Buyer education** | Helps buyers decide well | How to choose an IT partner, questions to ask vendors |
| **Founder journey** | Builds trust and relatability | Building the company, hiring, mistakes |
| **Industry point of view** | Makes you memorable | Opinions on trends, what the industry gets wrong |

## Step 2: set the mix

For 12 posts a month (3 a week), a balanced mix:

| Pillar | Share | Posts / month | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Expertise | 35% | 4 | Builds authority and saves |
| Buyer education | 25% | 3 | Attracts people who are actively buying |
| Founder journey | 25% | 3 | Drives comments and follows |
| Industry point of view | 15% | 2 | Drives reach and debate |

Adjust after 4 weeks: give more slots to the pillar with the best comments per 100 reactions and profile views.

## Step 3: plan the month

Example calendar for 12 posts (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday):

| Week | Tuesday | Wednesday | Thursday |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Expertise: how-to | Founder journey: story | Buyer education: mistake |
| 2 | Industry POV: contrarian | Expertise: client result | Founder journey: lesson |
| 3 | Buyer education: checklist | Expertise: myth vs reality | Founder journey: behind the scenes |
| 4 | Expertise: framework | Industry POV: prediction | Buyer education: question buyers ask |

## Step 4: fill the topic bank

Use [`topic-bank.csv`](topic-bank.csv): 60 ideas already organised by pillar. Replace the examples with the founder's own stories from the [voice interview](voice-interview.md). Aim to keep **20+ unused ideas** in the bank at all times.

## Format mix

| Format | When to use | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| Text only | Stories, opinions | Most posts. Fast to make |
| Text + photo | Founder journey, behind the scenes | Real photos beat stock |
| Carousel (PDF) | Checklists, frameworks, step-by-step | 6–10 slides, one idea per slide, big text |
| Poll | Quick debates | Use sparingly; follow up with a post on the result |
| Short video | Explaining one idea | Add captions; most people watch muted |
