# Voice guide: filled-in example

Write one of these after every voice interview, and check every draft against it. This example is for a **fictional founder**, so you can see what a finished guide looks like. Copy the blank template at the bottom.

---

## Founder: Arjun Mehta, CEO of CloudHarbor (fictional)

**Company:** Managed cloud and security for 50–500-person companies in the US
**Reader:** CTOs, IT heads and CFOs at mid-sized companies who are tired of surprise cloud bills and outages
**Goal:** Inbound sales conversations (target: 5 qualified DMs or calls a month by month 3)
**Desired action:** Visit the profile, follow, or DM "audit"

### Tone

| Is | Isn't |
|---|---|
| Calm, plain-spoken, a bit dry | Hype-y, salesy, "crushing it" |
| Confident but admits mistakes | Know-it-all |
| Practical: always an example or a number | Abstract theory |
| Respectful of in-house IT teams | Blaming the client's team |

### Sentence style

- Short sentences. Average 10–14 words.
- Uses "you" a lot; talks to one reader.
- Numbers instead of adjectives: "38% lower bill", not "much cheaper".
- Opens with the point, not the setup.
- No exclamation marks. Rarely uses emojis (✅ for lists only).

### Signature phrases (from the interview)

- "Nobody gets fired for a boring cloud bill."
- "Most outages are paperwork problems."
- "Fix the boring stuff first."
- "If you can't explain the bill, you don't control it."

### Words to avoid

synergy, leverage (as a verb), game-changer, revolutionary, cutting-edge, disrupt, "excited to announce", "humbled", "thought leader"

### Stories bank

| Story | Lesson | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Lost the first big client in 2017 after a 9-hour outage | Write the runbook before you need it | Story → lesson |
| A CFO asked "do we really need a security audit if nothing's broken?" | Nothing being broken is the risk | Buyer question |
| Moved a client off the cloud for one workload and saved 40% | Cloud isn't always the answer | Contrarian |
| Hired a senior engineer who failed; hired a junior who thrived | Hire for curiosity | Leadership |

### Opinions bank

- "Multi-cloud is mostly a sales pitch for small companies."
- "Your security audit should be boring. If it's exciting, something's wrong."
- "Most IT teams don't need more tools. They need fewer, used properly."

### Off-limits

- Client names without written permission
- Competitors by name
- Politics, family

### Formatting rules

- First line under 12 words
- 1–2 line paragraphs
- 0–2 hashtags, at the end
- Ends with a question or a clear next step
