# Engagement playbook

Posting is half the work. Founders who comment thoughtfully on other people's posts, and reply to every comment on their own, usually grow faster. This routine takes **20 minutes a day**.

## The daily 20-minute routine

| Minutes | Do this |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | Reply to every comment on your latest post (in the first hour after posting if you can) |
| 5–15 | Leave 3–5 thoughtful comments on posts by your buyers, peers and partners |
| 15–20 | Reply to DMs and connection requests from relevant people |

## Who to engage with

Build a list of **30–50 people** and check their posts a few times a week:

| Group | Why | How many |
|---|---|---|
| Ideal buyers (job titles you sell to) | They see your name and visit your profile | 15–25 |
| Peers and complementary founders | Their audiences overlap with yours | 10–15 |
| Industry voices with engaged audiences | Your comment reaches thousands of the right people | 5–10 |

**Tip:** ring the bell 🔔 on their profiles to get notified when they post.

## What makes a good comment

A good comment adds something: an example, a counterpoint, a question or a number. It's 2–4 sentences, and people should be able to tell it came from your experience.

| ❌ Weak | ✅ Strong |
|---|---|
| "Great post!" | "We saw the same thing with a client last month. Their bill dropped 30% after they tagged resources to owners. Ownership first, tools second." |
| "So true 🙌" | "Agree on the runbook point. One thing we added: a 'who to call' line at the very top. In a 3 am outage nobody reads past line one." |
| "Thanks for sharing." | "Curious how this works for teams under 20 people? We've found the overhead isn't worth it until about 50." |

## Comment templates

- **Add an example:** "We saw this with [type of client]. [What happened]. [What it taught us]."
- **Respectfully disagree:** "I'd push back a little on [point]. In our experience [counter-example]. Maybe it depends on [factor]?"
- **Ask a smart question:** "How do you handle [specific situation]? We've tried [X] and [Y]."
- **Add a number:** "Interesting. In our last [number] [projects], [stat]."

## Replying to comments on your own posts

- Reply to **every** comment in the first few hours. It keeps the conversation going.
- Answer with a question where you can: it turns one comment into a thread.
- Thank people by name for disagreeing. It shows confidence.
- If someone shows buying interest ("we have this problem"), reply publicly, then send a DM.

## DM etiquette (no spam)

- Never pitch in the first message.
- Reference something specific: "Saw your comment on my cloud costs post. Sounds like you're dealing with this right now?"
- Offer something useful first: a checklist, a quick answer, an intro.
- Move to a call only when they ask a question that needs one.
