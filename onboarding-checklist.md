# Onboarding checklist

Get everything you need in the first 48 hours, so writing can start on day 3.

## 1. Welcome email (send on day 1)

> **Subject:** Getting started: your LinkedIn content
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Excited to get started. To write posts that sound like you, I need three things this week:
>
> 1. **A 45-minute call** for the voice interview. Pick a time here: [booking link]. I'll record it so you don't have to repeat yourself later.
> 2. **5–10 things you've written or said**: old posts, emails to clients, a podcast or talk, even voice notes. The more casual, the better.
> 3. **Your goals and limits**: fill in the short form below (5 minutes).
>
> Once I have these, you'll get your first drafts within 5 working days.
>
> [Your name]

## 2. Intake form (copy into Google Forms or Typeform)

**About you**
- [ ] Full name, title, company, website
- [ ] LinkedIn profile URL
- [ ] One sentence: what does your company do, and for whom?
- [ ] Who is your ideal buyer? (job title, company size, industry, location)

**Goals** (pick up to two)
- [ ] Get inbound leads and sales conversations
- [ ] Build authority in a niche
- [ ] Recruit talent
- [ ] Attract investors or partners
- [ ] Support a launch or fundraise

**What success looks like in 90 days**
- [ ] e.g. "5 inbound DMs a month from operations leaders"

**Boundaries**
- [ ] Topics that are off-limits (clients, competitors, politics, family)
- [ ] Clients you can and can't mention by name
- [ ] Words or phrases you dislike
- [ ] Anyone who must approve posts (legal, co-founder, PR)

**Practical**
- [ ] Who posts: you, or me with access? (see access options below)
- [ ] Preferred approval channel: WhatsApp, Slack, email, Google Docs comments
- [ ] Time zone and best posting times
- [ ] Do you have LinkedIn Premium? (needed for the custom profile button)

## 3. Access options

| Option | How it works | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Founder posts** | You send approved posts as text; the founder copies and posts | Founders who want full control |
| **Scheduling tool** | Founder connects LinkedIn to a scheduler; you schedule approved posts | Busy founders (keeps password private) |
| **Shared password** | ❌ Avoid. It breaks LinkedIn's terms and creates security risk | — |

## 4. Kick-off deliverables (by day 5)

- [ ] Voice guide ([example](voice-guide-example.md))
- [ ] Profile recommendations ([checklist](profile-optimization.md))
- [ ] Content pillars and first 20 topics ([guide](content-pillars.md))
- [ ] First 3 drafts for approval
- [ ] Agreed posting days and times

## 5. Approval rules to agree up front

- **Turnaround:** founder replies within 24 hours, or posts go out as drafted? Agree on one.
- **Edit rounds:** one round of edits per post is standard; a second round for sensitive topics.
- **Fact checks:** the founder confirms every number, client name and claim before posting.
- **Emergencies:** pause all posts if there's breaking news, a crisis or a sensitive company event.
