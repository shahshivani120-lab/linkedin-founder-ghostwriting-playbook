# LinkedIn Founder Ghostwriting Playbook

A practical kit for writing LinkedIn posts **in a B2B founder's own voice**, posts that reach buyers outside their network and bring them to the founder's profile.

It's the process behind a ghostwriting week that got a US B2B IT founder **73.3K impressions in 7 days (+3,077%)**, **93% from outside his network**, and **+298% profile viewers**. [Read the case study (PDF)](https://shahshivani120-lab.github.io/case-studies/LinkedIn_Ghostwriting_Case_Study_Shivani_Shah.pdf).

## Why founder-led content works

Buyers trust people more than company pages. A founder's post gets shown to people who've never followed them, and that's where new buyers come from. The job of a ghostwriter is to make the founder's expertise easy to read **without losing their voice**.

## The 5-step process

| Step | What happens | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Voice** | A 45-minute interview on how the founder talks about their work, clients and running the company | [`voice-interview.md`](voice-interview.md) filled in, plus a voice cheat sheet |
| **2. Topics** | Turn their experience into a bank of post ideas buyers relate to | 20–30 ideas in [`topic-bank.csv`](topic-bank.csv) |
| **3. Hooks & drafts** | One idea per post, with a first line that earns the "see more" click | Drafts using [`hooks-and-templates.md`](hooks-and-templates.md) |
| **4. Publish** | Posts go out under the founder's name, in their voice | 3 posts a week is a good starting rhythm |
| **5. Learn** | Weekly review of impressions, comments, profile views and button clicks | [`weekly-tracker.csv`](weekly-tracker.csv) |

## What to measure (and why)

| Metric | Why it matters | Healthy signal |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions & members reached | Is LinkedIn showing the posts? | Reach growing week over week |
| % out-of-network | Are new people (potential buyers) seeing it? | Above 70% means posts are travelling |
| Comments per 100 reactions | Are people talking back, not just liking? | 30+ is good; 50+ is excellent |
| Saves and sends | Did people want to keep it or pass it on? | Any saves on a how-to or opinion post |
| Profile viewers | Are readers moving from the post to the person? | Rises after strong weeks |
| Custom button / link clicks | Buyer intent: they want to know more about the business | Track every week |
| Follower growth | Turns reach into a repeat audience | If reach is high but followers flat, add a follow CTA |

## Common mistakes

- **Writing like a brand page.** "We're excited to announce…" gets scrolled past. Write the way the founder speaks.
- **Product features instead of buyer problems.** Lead with the problem the buyer has, not what the product does.
- **One-week judgements.** Look at 4+ weeks before deciding what works.
- **No call to action.** High reach with flat follower growth usually means nobody was asked to follow or click.
