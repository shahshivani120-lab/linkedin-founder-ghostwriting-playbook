## The 5 principles behind it

1. **A person, not a logo.** People follow people. Posts come from the founder's name, opinions and stories.
2. **Buyer problems, not product features.** Every post should be useful to a buyer even if they never become a customer.
3. **One idea per post.** If you can't say the point in one sentence, it's two posts.
4. **Conversation over reach.** Comments and profile visits matter more than impressions alone.
5. **Review weekly, judge monthly.** One post or one week tells you little. Four weeks tell you what to repeat.
