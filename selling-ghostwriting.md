# Selling ghostwriting (for freelancers)

How to find, pitch and price founder ghostwriting clients.

## Who to target

The best clients are founders who:
- Sell to other businesses (B2B), with a deal size high enough that one client pays for months of content
- Have real expertise and opinions, but no time to write
- Already post occasionally (they believe in LinkedIn, but are inconsistent)

## Discovery call script (30 minutes)

**1. Open (2 min)**
> "Thanks for making time. I'd like to understand your goals and what you've tried, then I'll tell you honestly whether I can help. Sound good?"

**2. Situation (8 min)**
- "What does your company do, and who's your ideal client?"
- "How are you using LinkedIn today?"
- "What's worked? What hasn't?"

**3. Goals (8 min)**
- "If LinkedIn worked perfectly for you, what would happen in 6 months?"
- "Is that about leads, hiring, investors, or authority?"
- "How would you measure it?"

**4. Obstacles (5 min)**
- "What's stopped you from posting consistently so far?"
- "Have you worked with a writer before? How did it go?"
- "How do you feel about someone else writing in your voice?"

**5. Fit and next step (7 min)**
> "Here's how I work: one 45-minute voice interview, then I draft 3 posts a week. You spend about 30 minutes a week approving. In month one we fix your profile and find what resonates; by month three we're focused on what brings conversations."
>
> "Based on what you've told me, I think [package] fits. I'll send a short proposal today. Does that work?"

## Proposal outline (one page)

1. **Your goal** (in their words from the call)
2. **What I'll do** (voice interview, profile fixes, 12 posts a month, monthly report)
3. **What I need from you** (45-minute interview, 30 min/week approvals, monthly 20-minute call)
4. **Timeline** (first drafts within 5 working days)
5. **Investment** (package, price, payment terms)
6. **Proof** (1–2 results with numbers)
7. **Next step** (sign + book the voice interview)

## Example packages

| Package | Includes | Example price |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | 12 posts/month (3 a week), voice guide, profile review, monthly report | from US$500/month |
| **Growth** | Starter + 2 carousels/month + engagement support + comment drafting | higher tier |
| **Authority** | Growth + newsletter or long-form article monthly + DM strategy | premium tier |

Market rates vary widely: from a few hundred dollars a month for freelancers to several thousand for agencies. Price on the value of one new client for the founder, not on your hours.

## Terms worth including

- Minimum 3-month commitment (LinkedIn takes time)
- Payment monthly in advance
- One round of edits per post included
- Founder approves every post and confirms facts
- Either side can end with 30 days' notice after month 3
