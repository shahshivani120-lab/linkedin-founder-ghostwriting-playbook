# Editing checklist: 25 points

Run every draft through this before it goes to the founder. It takes 5 minutes and catches most problems.

## Hook (first 2–3 lines)
- [ ] 1. First line is under 12 words
- [ ] 2. It has tension: a number, a mistake, a contradiction or a question
- [ ] 3. Line 2 makes the reader want line 3
- [ ] 4. No warm-up ("In today's world…", "I've been thinking…", "Excited to share…")
- [ ] 5. The hook matches what the post delivers (no clickbait)

## Substance
- [ ] 6. The post makes **one** point. You can say it in one sentence.
- [ ] 7. There's at least one specific example, number or story
- [ ] 8. A buyer would find it useful even if they never become a customer
- [ ] 9. Every number and claim has been confirmed by the founder
- [ ] 10. No client named without written permission
- [ ] 11. It isn't a disguised sales pitch

## Voice
- [ ] 12. It sounds like the founder (read it aloud in their voice)
- [ ] 13. Uses at least one phrase or idea from the [voice guide](voice-guide-example.md)
- [ ] 14. No banned words from the voice guide
- [ ] 15. "I" for personal stories, "we" for the company, "you" for the reader

## Readability
- [ ] 16. Paragraphs are 1–2 lines
- [ ] 17. Sentences average under 15 words
- [ ] 18. No jargon a buyer wouldn't use (or it's explained)
- [ ] 19. Lists use → or numbers, not long sentences
- [ ] 20. Total length 120–250 words (longer only if every line earns its place)

## Ending and format
- [ ] 21. Ends with a real question or one clear next step
- [ ] 22. 0–3 relevant hashtags, at the end
- [ ] 23. External links go in the first comment, or are left out (posts with links tend to get less reach)
- [ ] 24. Tags only people who are genuinely involved
- [ ] 25. Spelling and grammar checked; reads well on a phone screen

## Quick rewrite tricks

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Weak hook | Use the most surprising sentence in the post as the first line |
| Too long | Delete the first paragraph, then cut every "really", "very", "actually", "just" |
| Too vague | Ask "for example?" and add the answer |
| Sounds generic | Add one detail only this founder could know |
| Weak ending | End with the question you'd ask the reader in person |
