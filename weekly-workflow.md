# Weekly workflow

A repeatable rhythm for 3 posts a week (12 a month). It takes the ghostwriter about 5–7 hours a week and the founder about 30 minutes.

## The week at a glance

| Day | Ghostwriter | Founder | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Pick next week's 3 topics from the bank; draft all 3 | — | 2–3 h |
| **Monday** | Publish post 1 of this week (approved last week) | Reply to comments in the first hour | 30 min |
| **Tuesday** | Edit drafts with the [checklist](editing-checklist.md); send for approval | — | 1 h |
| **Wednesday** | Publish post 2 | Approve or edit next week's drafts (15 min) + reply to comments | 30 min |
| **Thursday** | Make final edits; schedule next week's posts | — | 1 h |
| **Friday** | Publish post 3; log metrics in the [tracker](ghostwriting-tracker.xlsx); add new topics | Reply to comments; voice-note any new stories | 1 h |

**Always stay one week ahead.** Next week's posts should be approved by Thursday.

## Monthly rhythm

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | 20-minute check-in call: new stories, wins, upcoming events |
| Week 4 | Send the [monthly report](monthly-report-template.md); agree next month's focus |
| Every quarter | Re-run a short version of the [voice interview](voice-interview.md) (questions 7–20) |

## Approval messages (copy and paste)

**Sending drafts**
> Hi [Name], next week's 3 posts are here: [link]. Each has the hook first. Could you reply with ✅, or your edits, by Wednesday 6 pm? Two quick fact checks: (1) is the 28% figure right? (2) OK to mention the logistics client without naming them?

**Gentle reminder**
> Quick nudge on next week's posts. If I don't hear back by Thursday noon, I'll go ahead with posts 1 and 2 as drafted and hold post 3 (it has a client mention). Sound OK?

**Asking for stories**
> Anything interesting happen this week? A client question, a mistake, a win, a decision? A 60-second voice note is perfect. These make the best posts.

**After a strong post**
> Tuesday's post got 3x your usual comments, and 2 people asked about the audit in DMs. Worth replying to them today. I'll plan another post in the same style for next week.

## Drafting space: one doc per month

Keep a shared Google Doc per month with this structure, so the founder always knows where to look:

```
[Month] posts

## Week 1
### Post 1 · Tue [date] · Pillar: Expertise · Status: ✅ approved
[post text]
Founder notes:

### Post 2 · Wed [date] · ...
```

## When things go wrong

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Founder stops replying | Keep 3 "evergreen" approved posts in reserve to post while you wait |
| A post gets negative comments | Don't delete. Founder replies calmly once; ghostwriter drafts the reply if asked |
| Breaking news or crisis | Pause all scheduled posts immediately |
| Founder wants to post something off-plan | Great: edit it with the checklist and slot it in |
| A post flops | Normal. Judge pillars over 4 weeks, not single posts |
