# Hooks and post templates

The first line decides whether anyone clicks "see more". Each hook below is a pattern, not a script: rewrite it in the founder's words. Examples are illustrative, not real client results.

## Hook formulas

| Formula | Pattern | Example (IT services founder) |
|---|---|---|
| **The number** | [Specific number] + [surprising outcome] | "We cut a client's AWS bill by 38% without touching a single server." |
| **The mistake** | I/We got [X] wrong for [time]. | "I priced our first 20 projects wrong. Here's what it cost us." |
| **The contrarian** | Everyone says [X]. I disagree. | "Everyone says move everything to the cloud. For half our clients, that's bad advice." |
| **The question buyers ask** | "[Real question a client asked]" | "'Do we really need a security audit if nothing's broken?' A CFO asked me this last week." |
| **Before / after** | [Before state] → [after state]. Here's what changed. | "Three outages a month. Then zero for six months. One change made the difference." |
| **The lesson** | [Years] of [doing X] taught me [one thing]. | "12 years of running an IT company taught me that clients don't buy technology. They buy sleep." |
| **The list** | [Number] [things] I wish I knew before [event]. | "5 things I wish I'd known before hiring our first engineer." |

## Post structures

### 1. Story → lesson
```
[Hook: the moment]
[2–3 lines: what happened]
[1–2 lines: the turning point]
[The lesson, in one sentence]
[Question to the reader]
```

### 2. Opinion
```
[Hook: the contrarian claim]
[Why most people believe the opposite]
[What I've seen instead, with one example]
[When the common advice IS right (keeps it fair)]
[Question: "Where do you land on this?"]
```

### 3. How-to / checklist
```
[Hook: the outcome]
[Who this is for]
[Steps 1–5, one line each]
[The step people usually skip]
[CTA: "Save this for your next…"]
```

### 4. Client win (with permission)
```
[Hook: the result, with a number]
[The client's problem in their words]
[What we did: 3 bullets]
[What changed]
[The lesson for other buyers]
```

## Writing rules

- One idea per post.
- First line under 12 words. Second line makes them need the third.
- Short paragraphs: 1–2 lines each.
- Write the way the founder speaks, using phrases from the voice interview.
- No hashtags walls; 0–3 relevant ones at most.
- End with a real question, or a clear next step (follow, visit, DM).
- Ask: "Would a buyer find this useful even if they never hire us?"
