# Hook library: 60 formulas

The first 2–3 lines (roughly 200 characters) show before "see more". **Their only job is to make the reader click "see more".** Each formula below is a pattern, not a script: rewrite it in the founder's words.

*All examples are illustrative, written for a fictional IT services founder.*

## How to write a hook in 4 steps

1. **Write the post first.** Then find the most surprising sentence in it. That's usually the hook.
2. **Cut it to under 12 words.** Remove "I think", "In today's world", "Here's the thing".
3. **Add tension.** A number, a contradiction, a mistake or a question the reader wants answered.
4. **Write line 2 so they need line 3.** Line 2 should raise a question, not answer it.

## Hooks that don't work

| ❌ Weak | Why | ✅ Better |
|---|---|---|
| "Excited to share some thoughts on cloud costs." | No tension, no reason to read | "Our client was paying $14,000 a month for servers nobody used." |
| "In today's fast-paced digital world…" | Generic, could be anyone | "Most outages start with a spreadsheet." |
| "Here are my thoughts on security." | Says nothing | "Nothing being broken is the biggest security risk." |
| "Let's talk about hiring." | Vague | "I hired the wrong senior engineer. Then the right junior." |

## 1. Number hooks
1. "[Number] [unit] in [time]. Here's what changed." → *"$14,000 a month saved in 30 days. Nothing was switched off."*
2. "We [did X] for [number] clients. [Number] made the same mistake." → *"We've audited 120 cloud accounts. 97 had the same problem."*
3. "[Percentage] of [group] [surprising fact]." → *"Most companies we audit pay for 30% more servers than they use."*
4. "I spent [time/money] learning this so you don't have to." → *"I spent $40,000 learning this lesson about contracts."*
5. "[Number] years of [doing X]. [Number] lessons." → *"10 years of running an IT company. 10 lessons."*
6. "From [bad number] to [good number]." → *"From 3 outages a month to zero in six months."*

## 2. Mistake hooks
7. "I got [X] wrong for [time]." → *"I priced our first 20 projects wrong."*
8. "The most expensive mistake I've made as a founder:" → *"The most expensive mistake I've made: one client = 60% of revenue."*
9. "We lost a client because of [small thing]." → *"We lost a client because of a missing runbook."*
10. "Don't make the mistake I made with [X]." → *"Don't hire the way I hired in year one."*
11. "I almost [bad outcome]." → *"I almost shut the company down in 2019."*
12. "The [X] mistake I see every week." → *"The backup mistake I see every single week."*

## 3. Contrarian hooks
13. "Unpopular opinion: [claim]." → *"Unpopular opinion: your security audit should be boring."*
14. "Everyone says [X]. I disagree." → *"Everyone says move it all to the cloud. I disagree."*
15. "Stop [common practice]." → *"Stop buying more IT tools."*
16. "[Popular thing] is overrated." → *"Multi-cloud is overrated for small companies."*
17. "[Common belief] is wrong. Here's why." → *"'If it's not broken, don't fix it' is wrong in IT."*
18. "Hot take: [claim]." → *"Hot take: most outages are paperwork problems."*

## 4. Question hooks
19. "'[Real question a client asked]'" → *"'Do we really need a security audit if nothing's broken?'"*
20. "Why do [group] keep [doing X]?" → *"Why do smart companies keep overpaying for cloud?"*
21. "What would you do if [scenario]?" → *"What would you do if your servers went down right now?"*
22. "How much is [X] really costing you?" → *"How much is your 'free' migration really costing you?"*
23. "Can you answer this in 10 seconds: [question]?" → *"Can you name who gets paged when your site goes down?"*
24. "Is [X] worth it? Here's the honest answer." → *"Is a managed IT partner worth it? Honest answer:"*

## 5. Story hooks
25. "[Time] ago, [dramatic moment]." → *"Six years ago, I got a call at 3 am."*
26. "The day [event] changed how I [do X]." → *"The day we lost our biggest client changed how we work."*
27. "My first [X] was a disaster." → *"My first sales call was a disaster."*
28. "I'll never forget what [person] told me." → *"I'll never forget what my first client told me."*
29. "Last week a client [did/said X]." → *"Last week a CFO asked me to justify every line of their bill."*
30. "This [photo/email/message] changed everything." → *"This email from a client changed how we price."*

## 6. Lesson hooks
31. "[Time] taught me [one thing]." → *"12 years in IT taught me clients don't buy technology. They buy sleep."*
32. "The best advice I got about [X]:" → *"The best hiring advice I got: hire for curiosity."*
33. "What I wish I knew before [X]." → *"What I wish I knew before hiring our first engineer."*
34. "The one thing [successful group] do differently." → *"The one thing our best clients do differently."*
35. "A small habit that changed [X]." → *"A 15-minute Friday habit that prevents most IT fires."*
36. "[X] is simple. It's just not easy." → *"Good security is simple. It's just not exciting."*

## 7. How-to hooks
37. "How to [outcome] in [time]:" → *"How to read your AWS bill in 10 minutes:"*
38. "The [number]-step [process] we use for [outcome]." → *"The 5-step checklist we run with every new client."*
39. "Steal this [template/checklist]." → *"Steal this incident post-mortem template."*
40. "If you [situation], do this first." → *"If your cloud bill jumped this month, check this first."*
41. "A simple way to [outcome] without [pain]." → *"A simple way to cut cloud costs without slowing anything down."*
42. "[Number] questions to ask before [decision]." → *"5 questions to ask before signing an IT contract."*

## 8. Before / after hooks
43. "Before: [bad state]. After: [good state]." → *"Before: 3 outages a month. After: 0 in six months."*
44. "We used to [X]. Now we [Y]." → *"We used to fix problems. Now we prevent them."*
45. "What changed between [bad time] and [good time]?" → *"What changed between our worst year and our best?"*
46. "Same [X], different [result]." → *"Same servers. Half the bill."*

## 9. Behind-the-scenes hooks
47. "What a [role]'s week actually looks like." → *"What an IT company founder's week actually looks like."*
48. "Here's exactly how we [do X]." → *"Here's exactly how we onboard a new client."*
49. "The [document/tool] we use every day." → *"The one-page doc every client gets in week one."*
50. "Things nobody tells you about [running X]." → *"Things nobody tells you about running an IT company."*
51. "A look inside [process/meeting]." → *"A look inside our Monday incident review."*
52. "The real numbers behind [X]." → *"The real numbers behind our first 5 years."*

## 10. Pattern-interrupt hooks
53. "This post will annoy some [group]." → *"This post will annoy some IT vendors."*
54. "Nobody talks about this." → *"Nobody talks about how boring good security is."*
55. "I'm going to say what [group] won't." → *"I'm going to say what most vendors won't."*
56. "[Short shocking statement]." → *"We fired our biggest client."*
57. "Read this before you [action]." → *"Read this before you sign your next IT contract."*
58. "Quick one: [simple question]." → *"Quick one: who owns your company's passwords?"*
59. "[Thing] isn't the problem. [Other thing] is." → *"The cloud isn't the problem. Nobody owning it is."*
60. "If you remember one thing from this week, make it this:" → *"If you remember one thing this week: test your backups."*

## Line 2 formulas (make them read line 3)

| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| Raise the stakes | "And it cost us our biggest client." |
| Promise the answer | "Here's what we found, and how to fix it." |
| Add a twist | "The fix took 20 minutes." |
| Narrow it to the reader | "If you run IT for a 50–500-person company, this is for you." |
| Admit something | "I didn't see it coming." |
