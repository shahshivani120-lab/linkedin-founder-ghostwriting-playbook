# Founder voice interview

A 45-minute recorded call. **Don't ask for content ideas.** Ask for stories and opinions; the posts come out of the answers. Write down the founder's exact words; they become the voice.

**Before the call:** read their website, 10 of their past posts and any talks or podcasts. Note phrases they repeat.
**During the call:** ask "Can you give me an example?" after every general answer. Examples become posts.
**After the call:** transcribe it (any transcription tool works), highlight stories, opinions and phrases, and write the [voice guide](voice-guide-example.md).

---

## Part 1 · The business in their words (8 min)

| # | Question | Why you're asking | Good follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How do you explain what you do to someone at a dinner party? | Their natural, jargon-free pitch | "What do they usually ask next?" |
| 2 | Who's your best customer? Describe one real one. | Defines the reader | "What was going on in their business when they found you?" |
| 3 | What problem were they trying to solve? | Buyer pain = post topics | "What had they already tried?" |
| 4 | What do customers say about you that surprised you? | Real differentiators | "Who said that?" |
| 5 | What do you do differently from the obvious competitors? | Positioning | "Why don't they do it too?" |
| 6 | What's the most common mistake buyers make before they hire you? | Educational posts | "What does that mistake cost them?" |

## Part 2 · Origin and stories (12 min)

| # | Question | Why | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | Why did you start the company? What was the moment? | Origin story post | "What did you almost do instead?" |
| 8 | Tell me about a project that went wrong. | Lesson posts (high engagement) | "What would you do differently now?" |
| 9 | Tell me about a client win you're proud of. | Proof posts | "What number changed?" |
| 10 | What decision did people around you disagree with? | Contrarian posts | "Were you right?" |
| 11 | What did your first year teach you? | "What I wish I knew" posts | "What would you tell a founder in year one?" |
| 12 | Who shaped how you work? A boss, mentor, parent? | Personal, relatable posts | "What did they say that stuck?" |
| 13 | What's the hardest hire or firing you've done? | Leadership posts | "What did you learn about hiring?" |
| 14 | What's a small habit that makes a big difference in your business? | Practical tips | "How did you start doing it?" |

## Part 3 · Opinions (12 min)

| # | Question | Why | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | What does your industry get wrong? | Strong opinion posts | "Why does it keep happening?" |
| 16 | What popular advice do you think is bad? | Contrarian hooks | "When IS it good advice?" |
| 17 | What trend is overhyped? What's underrated? | Timely posts | "What are you doing about it?" |
| 18 | If a buyer could ask one question before choosing a vendor, what should it be? | Buyer guides | "What answer should they listen for?" |
| 19 | What do you believe that most people in your industry don't? | Signature idea | "What proof do you have?" |
| 20 | What will your industry look like in 5 years? | Vision posts | "Who loses?" |

## Part 4 · How they talk (8 min)

| # | Question | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 21 | Formal or casual? Short and punchy or detailed? | Sentence style |
| 22 | Any phrases you use a lot? | Signature phrases |
| 23 | Words you'd never use? (e.g. "synergy", "game-changer") | Banned words list |
| 24 | Emojis: yes, no, sometimes? | Formatting rules |
| 25 | Whose LinkedIn content do you like? Why? | Style references |
| 26 | Whose content do you dislike? Why? | What to avoid |

## Part 5 · Goals and limits (5 min)

| # | Question | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 27 | Who do you most want reading your posts? | Target reader |
| 28 | What should they do after reading? (follow, DM, visit, book a call) | Calls to action |
| 29 | What's off-limits? | Boundaries |
| 30 | What would make this a success in 90 days? | Shared definition of success |

## After the call: turn answers into posts

Every good answer is a post. As a rule of thumb, one 45-minute interview produces 20–30 post ideas.

| Answer type | Becomes | Framework |
|---|---|---|
| A story with a turning point | Story → lesson post | [Framework 1](post-frameworks.md#1-story--lesson) |
| A strong opinion | Contrarian post | [Framework 2](post-frameworks.md#2-contrarian-opinion) |
| "The mistake buyers make…" | Buyer guide | [Framework 3](post-frameworks.md#3-buyer-mistake) |
| A client result with a number | Proof post | [Framework 5](post-frameworks.md#5-client-result) |
| A habit or process | How-to post | [Framework 4](post-frameworks.md#4-how-to--checklist) |

Log every idea straight into [`topic-bank.csv`](topic-bank.csv) with the question number as the source.
