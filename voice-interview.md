# Founder voice interview

Run this as a 45-minute recorded call. Don't ask for "content ideas": ask for stories and opinions, and the posts come from the answers. Note the exact words the founder uses; those become the voice.

## 1. The business, in their words (10 min)
1. How do you explain what your company does to someone at a dinner party?
2. Who's your best customer? Describe one real one.
3. What problem were they trying to solve when they found you?
4. What do customers say about you that you didn't expect?
5. What do you do differently from the obvious competitors?

## 2. Stories (15 min)
6. Why did you start the company? What was the moment you decided?
7. Tell me about a project that went wrong. What did you learn?
8. Tell me about a client win you're proud of. What made it work?
9. What's a decision you made that people around you disagreed with?
10. What did the first year teach you that you wish you'd known earlier?

## 3. Opinions (10 min)
11. What does your industry get wrong?
12. What advice do you hear a lot that you think is bad?
13. What trend are people overhyping right now? What are they underrating?
14. If a buyer could only ask you one question before choosing a vendor, what should it be?

## 4. How they talk (5 min)
15. Phrases you use a lot? Words you'd never use?
16. Formal or casual? Short punchy sentences or longer explanations?
17. Topics that are off-limits (clients, competitors, personal life)?
18. Founders or creators on LinkedIn whose style you like?

## 5. Wrap-up (5 min)
19. Who do you most want to read your posts? (role, company size, location)
20. What would you like them to do after reading? (follow, visit the website, book a call, DM)
